14 looks

Not a color filter. A whole camera.

Each look recreates a kind of camera: its film or sensor — color, grain, digital noise — and its lens — softness, vignette, distortion, flare and light leaks.

35mm film

Cinema film at night and the compacts people carried in the 90s.

Tungsten 800Night cinema film. Red halos around lights, teal shadows.
Titan 38Premium 90s compact. Crisp, cool shadows, warm highlights.
Mju 35Pocket point-and-shoot in black and white. Hard contrast, big grain.

Disposable

The party camera from the bottom of the bag.

Snap 400Soft plastic lens, rainbow flare, greenish shadows.
Snap GoldSummer disposable. Golden, saturated, coarser grain.

Instant

Prints that develop in your hand, square or pocket-sized.

SX FoldFolding instant. Glassy, deep blue shadows, lilac highlights.
Square 600Boxy instant. Soft, green shadows, pink highlights.
Mini InstantCredit-card size. Clear, bright, a little cool.

Toy camera

Plastic lenses, dark corners and light that sneaks in.

Tunnel 35Tunnel vignette, cross-processed color, a leak at the edge.
Toy 120Medium-format toy. Dark corners, blur toward the edges.
Dream 120Very soft, glowing highlights, a pink light leak.
