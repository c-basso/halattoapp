How it works

From phone photo to film print in three steps

Halatto is an editor first: it works on photos you already took, and keeps every edit so you can come back and change the look.

  1. 1

    Open a photo

    Pick one from your library, or in Photos tap Share and choose Halatto. Or shoot a new one with the built-in camera.

  2. 2

    Pick a camera

    Choose one of 14 looks, then set its strength, grain and film age. Add aging, light leaks, flash and the date stamp if you like.

  3. 3

    Save or share

    Save the photo, share it, or export a before-and-after video. Save the edit as a recipe to use it again.

Tip: let one look carry a whole set. A trip or a party feels like one roll of film when every photo shares a camera.

FAQ

Vintage photo filter questions, answered

Short answers first. Each one links to a full step-by-step guide.

How do I make my iPhone photos look like film?

Open the photo in Halatto, pick a film look such as Titan 38 or Tungsten 800, and raise grain and film age a little. Film color, grain and the softness of an old lens are what make a photo read as film; a color filter alone does not.

Learn more

Is there a disposable camera filter for photos I already took?

Yes. Most disposable camera apps only work when you shoot in them. In Halatto you open any photo from your library and choose Snap 400 or Snap Gold, then add the flash and the orange date stamp.

Learn more

How do I get the Y2K digicam look on iPhone?

Pick one of the three digicam looks (Silver IX, Cyber Blue or Digi 2MP) and add the Digital flash. They add CCD color, sensor noise, cool whites and the hard flash of a 2000s compact camera.

Learn more

How do I add a date stamp to a photo on iPhone?

The Photos app cannot print a date on a picture. In Halatto open the photo, tap Date stamp and choose a corner: the orange date is taken from the day the photo was shot, and you can change its color, size and direction.

Learn more

How can I make a photo look old?

Use Aging: start with the Shoebox or Faded print preset, then raise Fade and Yellowing and add dust, stains or creases. A warm film look and a light leak finish the effect.

Learn more

Can I get a Polaroid-style instant photo look?

Yes. Halatto has three instant looks: SX Fold for the classic folding-camera color, Square 600 for the boxy instant look and Mini Instant for credit-card prints. Halatto is not affiliated with Polaroid or Fujifilm.

Learn more

How do I add film grain to a photo on iPhone?

Every Halatto look has its own grain, and you can set the amount after you pick it. Grain looks most natural with a little fade and a softer lens, which the film looks add for you.

Learn more

How do I put the same filter on many photos at once?

Save your edit as a recipe, then use Develop the Roll: select the photos and tap Develop all to put the same camera on every one, so a trip looks like one roll of film.

Learn more

Does Halatto upload my photos?

No. Photos are edited on your iPhone, there is no account, and nothing is uploaded to a server.

Learn more

Is Halatto free, and which devices does it run on?

It is free to download, and every look and effect works up to a daily limit. A subscription or a lifetime purchase removes the limit. It requires iOS 18.6 or later on iPhone; there is no Android version.

Learn more
