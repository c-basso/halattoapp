Apps like Dazz Cam for iPhone, for photos you already took

Updated November 21, 2026, 5 min read

Quick answer

Dazz Cam is built around shooting: you pick a simulated camera and take a new photo. If you mostly shoot new photos, Dazz and apps like Huji or NOMO CAM fit. If you want the same old-camera look on photos already in your library, with aging, light leaks, a date stamp and one look on a whole set, use an editor such as Halatto.

People look for apps like Dazz Cam for different reasons: the app is not on their phone, they want something different, or they want the look on photos they took earlier with the normal iPhone camera.

Here is how the options differ, so you can pick by what you actually do with your photos. We make Halatto, so read our part with that in mind.

What people like about Dazz Cam

  • A choice of simulated old cameras instead of plain color filters.
  • Film and digicam looks in one app.
  • Light leaks, grain and date stamps that feel like a real print.
  • The fun of picking a camera before you shoot.

When an editor fits better than a camera app

  • You took the photos with the normal iPhone camera and only later wanted a film look.
  • You want to age the picture: fade, yellowing, dust, stains and creases.
  • You want one look on a whole set, so a trip reads like one roll.
  • You want a before-and-after video of the edit for Stories or Reels.
  • You prefer that photos stay on the iPhone with no account.

Tips

  • Using both is normal: a camera app for nights out, an editor for the camera roll.
  • Test any app on a night photo with people; that is where looks differ most.
  • For a set, pick one camera and stick to it.

Questions people also ask

Is there a free app like Dazz Cam?

Most apps in this category are free to download with paid extras. Halatto is free to try: every look and effect works up to a daily limit, and a subscription or lifetime purchase removes the limit.

Can I use a Dazz-style filter on photos I already took?

Yes, with an editor. In Halatto you open any photo from your library, including old ones and screenshots, and pick a camera look.

Is Halatto affiliated with Dazz Cam?

No. Halatto is an independent app. Dazz Cam is named here only to describe the kind of look people search for.

Is there an Android app like Dazz Cam from Halatto?

No. Halatto is iPhone only and requires iOS 18.6 or later.
