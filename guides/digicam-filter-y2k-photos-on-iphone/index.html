Digital camera filter: the old Y2K digicam look on iPhone

By Vladimir Ivakhnenko · Updated November 26, 2026, 5 min read

Quick answer

The Y2K digicam look comes from small CCD sensors and a hard built-in flash: punchy or cool color, digital noise, blown highlights and a slightly smeared, low-resolution JPEG. In Halatto, open the photo, pick Silver IX (warm, pink skin), Cyber Blue (blue whites, contrast) or Digi 2MP (early CCD with JPEG blocks and light streaks), then add the Digital flash.

Early-2000s compact digital cameras are back because they look nothing like a modern phone: the flash is harsh, the white balance is off, there is noise in every shadow and the JPEG is a little crunchy.

You do not need to buy a used camera on eBay. Here is how to get the same result on any iPhone photo.

What makes a photo look like a 2000s digicam

  • CCD color: saturated reds, cool or magenta-tinted whites, odd skin tones.
  • Noise: colored grain in shadows, more at night.
  • Flash: bright subject, dark background, a shine on faces.
  • Low resolution: soft detail, JPEG blocks, purple fringing on edges, vertical streaks from bright lights.

Tips for the Y2K look

  • Night and indoor photos with people in the foreground work best; landscapes look less authentic.
  • Crop to 4:3, the shape of most old digicam sensors.
  • Digi 2MP is strongest; use a lower strength on faces.
  • Keep a whole night consistent with one look and Develop the Roll.

Questions people also ask

How do I get the digital camera look on iPhone?

Open the photo in Halatto, pick Silver IX, Cyber Blue or Digi 2MP and add the Digital flash. For a softer, dreamy digital camera look, use Silver IX at lower strength and skip the flash.

What is a CCD camera filter?

It imitates the color and noise of CCD sensors used in compact cameras around 2000 to 2010. Halatto's digicam looks are built on that idea.

Is a digicam filter the same as a disposable filter?

No. Disposables are film: grain, soft plastic lens, warm or green color. Digicams are digital: noise, cool whites, crunchy JPEG detail and a hard flash.

Can I use the digicam look on old photos?

Yes, any photo from your library works, including screenshots and saved images.
