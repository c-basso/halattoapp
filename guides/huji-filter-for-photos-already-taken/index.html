Huji-style filter for photos you already took

By Vladimir Ivakhnenko · Updated November 27, 2026, 5 min read

Quick answer

Huji Cam is a camera app: you shoot in it and get one 1998 disposable-camera look with light streaks and a date. It can also process photos from your library, as a paid extra. If you want that kind of look on photos you already took with more control, open them in an editor such as Halatto: pick Snap Gold or Snap 400, add a light leak and the orange date stamp, and put the same look on a whole set at once.

The usual story: you took the photos with the normal iPhone camera, and only later wanted them to look like a late-90s disposable. A camera app is built for the moment of shooting, so the question becomes what to do with the camera roll.

Here is what Huji Cam does with existing photos, and how to get a similar look in an editor. We make Halatto, so read our part with that in mind.

What the Huji look is made of

  • Warm, saturated color like a summer disposable.
  • Visible grain and a slightly soft plastic lens.
  • Random streaks of colored light across the frame.
  • A date in the corner, in the style of a 90s camera back.

Can Huji Cam edit photos you already took?

Yes, with a condition. Huji Cam is camera-first: its main screen is a small viewfinder and you take new pictures in it. Importing photos from your library is part of its in-app purchase, not the free app.

An imported photo gets the same single look as a new shot, including the light effect and the date. If that one look is what you want, the import is the shortest way.

Checked against the Huji Cam App Store listing in November 2026. Apps change; look at the current listing before you pay.

When an editor fits better

  • You want to choose the camera: a disposable, a 35mm compact, an instant, a toy camera or a Y2K digicam.
  • You want to set how strong the look is, and how much grain and film age it has.
  • You want to decide where the light leak goes and what color it is, or leave it out.
  • You want the date stamp to show the day the photo was taken.
  • You want one look on a whole trip or party at once.

Step by step: the 90s disposable look on a photo you already took

About a minute in Halatto.

  1. 1

    Open the photo

    Open Halatto and pick a photo from your library, or in Photos tap Share and choose Halatto.

  2. 2

    Pick Snap Gold or Snap 400

    Swipe the Looks strip and compare with the Before/After divider. Snap Gold suits daylight, Snap 400 suits evenings and parties.

  3. 3

    Add a light leak

    Open Light Leak, choose Edge or Burn and a soft red or orange preset, then set Amount and Position.

  4. 4

    Stamp the date

    Open Date stamp and choose a corner. The orange date is taken from the day the photo was shot.

  5. 5

    Do the whole set

    Save the edit as a recipe, then use Develop the Roll to put it on the rest of the photos.

Tips

  • Keep the leak at the edge of the frame; a streak across a face reads as a mistake.
  • Daylight photos take a disposable look best. For night shots, add the Retro flash.
  • Using both kinds of app is normal: a camera app for the night out, an editor for the camera roll.

Questions people also ask

Can I use the Huji filter on photos I already took?

Huji Cam can process photos from your library, but importing is part of its in-app purchase. To get a similar 90s disposable look with more control, open the photo in an editor such as Halatto and use Snap Gold or Snap 400 with a light leak and the date stamp.

Is there a free way to get this look on existing photos?

Halatto is free to try: every look and effect works up to a daily limit, and a subscription or lifetime purchase removes the limit.

Can I put the look on many photos at once?

Yes. In Halatto, save the edit as a recipe and use Develop the Roll to apply it to a whole set.
