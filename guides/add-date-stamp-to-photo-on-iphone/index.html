How to add a date stamp to a photo on iPhone, even one already taken

By Vladimir Ivakhnenko · Updated November 26, 2026, 5 min read

Quick answer

The iPhone Camera and Photos apps store the date in the photo's info but cannot print it on the picture. In Halatto, open the photo, tap Date stamp and pick a corner: the orange date is read from the day the photo was taken, in the '26 11 19 style of film cameras. The orange date in the corner is the most recognizable detail of a 90s film photo. Point-and-shoot cameras exposed it onto the film with tiny LEDs, so it glows slightly and sits on top of the picture.

Here is how to get that exact look on an iPhone photo, using its real capture date.

Can the iPhone add a date stamp on its own?

No. Swipe up on a photo in Photos (or tap the i button) and you will see the date and time, which you can also adjust. That information lives in the file's metadata; it is not drawn on the image, and it disappears in many apps and screenshots.

To make the date part of the picture you need an app that draws it on. Utility apps put a plain timestamp on photos for documentation. Halatto draws the retro orange one, matched to film looks.

What a real film date stamp looks like

  • Format: 'YY MM DD with an apostrophe, for example '26 11 19.
  • Color: glowing orange-red, a little soft at the edges.
  • Position: usually the bottom right corner, sometimes rotated on vertical shots.

Step by step: date stamp in Halatto

Thirty seconds.

  1. 1

    Open the photo

    Pick the photo in Halatto. Optionally choose a film look first so the date sits on film color.

  2. 2

    Open Date stamp

    Tap Date stamp and choose one of the four corners. The off icon removes it.

  3. 3

    Set direction

    Use Direction for a horizontal date, or a vertical one going up or down along the edge.

  4. 4

    Adjust color and size

    Drag Color to move from classic orange toward yellow or green, and Size to make it smaller or bigger.

  5. 5

    Save

    Tap the checkmark and save or share the photo.

Tips

  • Keep the date small; on old prints it was a few millimetres high.
  • Bottom right on a dark area of the photo glows best.
  • Pair it with Snap 400 or Titan 38 for a 90s point-and-shoot look.
  • Stamp every photo of a trip the same way so the set reads like one roll of prints.

Questions people also ask

Can I add a date stamp to a photo already taken?

Yes. Open any photo from your library in Halatto and tap Date stamp. The date comes from the day the photo was taken, so a picture from last summer gets last summer's date, not today's.

Which date does Halatto use?

The day the photo was taken, read from the photo's information.

Can I add a date stamp to a screenshot or saved image?

Yes. Any image in your library works; the date comes from the information saved with it.

Can I remove the date stamp later?

Yes. Reopen the edit in Halatto, open Date stamp and turn it off.
