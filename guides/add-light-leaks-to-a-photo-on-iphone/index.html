Light leaks

How to add a light leak effect to a photo on iPhone

Updated November 21, 2026, 5 min read

Quick answer

A light leak is the warm glow from light that got onto the film by accident. In Halatto, open the photo, tap Light Leak, choose a preset (Soft red, Orange, Golden), pick the type (Edge for a glow from one side, Window for a soft patch, Burn for a strong blown-out flare), then set Color, Amount and Position. Or pick a toy camera look, such as Tunnel 35 or Dream 120, that already leaks.

Light leaks came from cheap or old cameras with gaps in the body, from opening the back too early, or from the end of a roll. Today they are the quickest way to make a photo feel warm and nostalgic.

The trick is to keep them subtle and put them where light could actually get in: the edge of the frame.

Edge, Window or Burn?

TypeWhat it looks likeUse it for
EdgeA glow coming in from one side of the frame.Most photos; the classic leak.
WindowA soft patch of light over part of the image.Dreamy portraits, flowers, golden hour.
BurnA strong, blown-out flare as if the film was exposed.End-of-roll frames, music and party photos.

Toy cameras that leak by design

  • Tunnel 35: tunnel vignette, cross-processed color, a leak at the edge.
  • Toy 120: dark corners and blur toward the edges.
  • Dream 120: very soft, glowing highlights and a pink light leak.

Step by step: light leak in Halatto

About a minute.

  1. 1

    Pick a film look

    A leak looks best on film color, so start with a look such as Snap Gold or Titan 38.

  2. 2

    Open Light Leak

    Choose Soft red, Orange or Golden.

  3. 3

    Choose the type

    Tap Edge, Window or Burn.

  4. 4

    Place it

    Set Color, keep Amount low, and move Position so the leak comes from an edge, away from faces.

  5. 5

    Save

    Tap the checkmark, then save or share.

Tips

  • One leak per photo. Several at once look like stickers.
  • Warm leaks (orange, golden) suit sunsets; soft red suits night and neon.
  • In a set, vary the leak's position a little so each frame looks individual.

Questions people also ask

Are light leaks a mistake?

Originally yes, now mostly a style. They signal film and imperfection, which is why they are popular on social media.

Can I remove a light leak later?

Yes. Reopen the edit in Halatto and set Light Leak back to Original.

Do light leaks work on black and white photos?

Yes; on black and white they show as a bright, washed-out glow.
