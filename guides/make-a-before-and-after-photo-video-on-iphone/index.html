How to make a before and after photo video on iPhone

Updated November 21, 2026, 5 min read

Quick answer

After you edit a photo in Halatto, open Export and choose the video option: a divider sweeps across the photo from the original to the film look. Pick Original, 9:16 (Stories, Reels, TikTok), 4:5 (feed) or 1:1, and tap Share video. Doing the same in a video editor means exporting two images and animating a mask by hand.

Before-and-after reveals are some of the most-watched photo posts: people want to see what the edit changed.

Here is the quick way to make one on iPhone from a vintage edit, and how to format it for each platform.

Which aspect ratio to export

Where you postAspect ratio
Instagram Stories, Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts9:16

Keep the photo's own shapeOriginal

The manual way, for comparison

Without a built-in export you would save the original and the edited photo, put both on a timeline in a video editor such as CapCut or iMovie, add a wipe transition or animate a mask, then export at the right size. It works, but it takes minutes per photo.

Step by step: reveal video in Halatto

Under a minute after the edit.

    Edit the photo

  2. 2

Tips for reveals that get watched

  • Choose photos where the change is obvious: a sharp, cold phone photo turned warm and grainy.
  • Put the look's name in your caption; people ask which filter it is.
  • Post a carousel of the final photos after the reveal.

Questions people also ask

Can I make a before and after video in the Photos app?

Not directly. Photos can compare an edit by tapping it, but it cannot export a reveal video.

Does the video include a watermark?

The App Store screenshot of the export shows a small Halatto mark in the corner of the video.

Which apps can I share the video to?

Any app in the iOS share sheet, including Instagram, TikTok, Messages and Photos.
