Best vintage and film camera apps for iPhone

Updated November 21, 2026, 5 min read

Quick answer

Pick by how you work. If you want to shoot with the look on and enjoy a disposable-style ritual, a camera-first app like Huji Cam, Dazz Cam or Dispo fits. If you want deep film color grading, VSCO or RNI Films. If you want to turn photos you already took into film or old-camera shots, with aging, light leaks, a date stamp and batch editing, use an editor like Halatto.

The App Store has hundreds of vintage camera apps, and most look alike in screenshots. The real difference is what they are built around: shooting, grading or editing existing photos.

This guide sorts them by that, so you can pick the right kind before comparing individual filters. We make Halatto, so read our take on it with that in mind.

What to check before you install

  • Does it edit photos you already took, or only new shots?
  • Is the look a color filter, or does it add grain, lens softness and leaks?
  • Can you put one look on many photos?
  • Does it need an account or upload your photos?
  • What is free: a few filters, or everything with a limit?

Privacy: where your photos go

Some vintage and AI photo apps process images on a server. Halatto edits on your iPhone, with no account, and nothing is uploaded. Its App Store privacy label lists only purchases, identifiers, usage data and diagnostics, none linked to you.

Where Halatto fits

Halatto is an editor first: open any photo, pick one of 14 old cameras (35mm, disposable, instant, toy, Y2K digicam), then age it, leak it, flash it and date it. Recipes and Develop the Roll put one look on a whole trip, and the before-and-after video export is made for stories. A built-in camera covers new shots.

Every look and effect is free up to a daily limit; a subscription or lifetime purchase removes the limit.

Tips for choosing

  • Test any app on a night photo and a portrait; that is where cheap filters fall apart.
  • If you like the look on your existing camera roll, you do not need to change how you shoot.
  • Many people use both: a camera app for nights out, an editor for everything else.

Questions people also ask

What is the best app to make photos look like film?

If you want to edit photos you already took, pick an editor such as Halatto. If you want to shoot new photos with a film look, a camera-first app like Huji or Dazz fits better. Both kinds are covered above.

What is the best Huji Cam alternative?

If you want to keep shooting in-app, Dazz Cam or NOMO CAM. If you want the disposable look on photos you already took, an editor like Halatto.

Are there apps like Dazz Cam for iPhone?

Yes. Huji, Dispo and Halatto all give an old-camera look. Dazz is camera-first; Halatto works on photos already in your library and keeps everything on the iPhone.

Are vintage camera apps free?

Most are free to download with paid filters or subscriptions. Halatto gives every look up to a daily limit for free.

Is there an Android version of Halatto?

No. Halatto is iPhone only and requires iOS 18.6 or later.
