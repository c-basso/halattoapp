Batch edit

How to apply the same filter to multiple photos on iPhone

Updated November 21, 2026, 5 min read

Quick answer

In Photos, edit one photo, tap the More button and choose Copy Edits, then select the other photos, tap More and Paste Edits. That copies Photos adjustments only. For a film look on a whole set, edit one photo in Halatto and Save as Recipe, or open Develop the Roll, choose a camera, select the photos and tap Develop all.

A trip, a party or a feed looks finished when every photo shares the same color and texture, like one roll of film. Editing thirty photos one by one is how most people give up.

Here are the two batch options on iPhone and when each one fits.

Option 1: Copy and Paste Edits in Photos

  1. Open an edited photo in Photos.
  2. Tap the More button (three dots) and choose Copy Edits.
  3. Go back, tap Select and choose the other photos.
  4. Tap the More button and choose Paste Edits.

This copies Photos adjustments and filters only. It cannot add grain, light leaks, a date stamp or a film camera look.

Option 2: recipes and Develop the Roll in Halatto

A recipe is your full edit saved under a name: the look, its strength, grain and film age, aging, leaks, flash and date stamp. Apply it to any photo later in one tap.

Develop the Roll is the batch version: choose a camera, select the photos, and Develop all puts the look on every one, like getting a whole roll back from the lab.

Step by step: one look for a whole trip

A couple of minutes for a few dozen photos.

  1. 1

    Edit one hero photo

    Open your favorite photo of the set and build the look: camera, grain, aging, leak, date.

  2. 2

    Save as Recipe

    Save the edit as a recipe and give it a name, for example Lisbon 2026.

  3. 3

    Open Develop the Roll

    Pick the camera for the roll and select the rest of the photos.

  4. 4

    Develop all

    Tap Develop all. Every selected photo gets the same look.

  5. 5

    Fine-tune and share

    Reopen any single photo to adjust it, then save or share the set.

Tips

  • Pick the hero photo with the most typical light of the trip, not the most extreme one.
  • Night and day photos may need two recipes from the same camera.
  • Use one camera per album; switching cameras mid-set breaks the roll feeling.

Questions people also ask

Can I batch edit photos in the iPhone Photos app?

Yes, with Copy Edits and Paste Edits, but only Photos' own adjustments are copied.

How many photos can Develop the Roll handle?

A whole set at once; the button shows how many photos are selected.

Can I use a recipe on photos from another trip?

Yes. Recipes stay in the app, so the same look can go on any photo, any time.
