How to make a photo look old on iPhone

By Vladimir Ivakhnenko · Updated November 26, 2026, 5 min read

Quick answer

An old photo is faded, yellowed and physically worn. In Halatto, pick a warm film look first, then open Aging and start from the Shoebox or Faded print preset. Raise Fade and Yellowing, add some Unevenness, then dust, stains or creases and a light leak at the edge. The Photos app can only lower contrast and warm the color; it cannot add wear.

"Vintage" covers two different things: the color of old film, and the damage time does to a print. The best old-photo effects combine both.

This guide shows the settings to change and the order to apply them so the result looks aged, not just filtered.

The signs of an old photo

  • Fade: blacks turn gray and contrast drops.
  • Yellowing: whites drift warm and the whole print turns amber.
  • Uneven color: one side faded more than the other.
  • Wear: dust specks, stains, creases and bent corners.
  • Light leaks: a warm glow bleeding in from the edge of the film.

Making it look old for free in Photos

In Photos, tap Edit, then lower Contrast and Saturation, raise Warmth and Shadows, and lower Black Point. You can also try the Silvertone filter for a black and white print. This fades the photo, but there is no way to add dust, stains, creases or leaks.

Step by step: an old photo in Halatto

About two minutes.

  1. 1

    Open the photo and pick a look

    Choose a warm film look (Snap Gold, Titan 38) or Mju 35 for black and white.

  2. 2

    Age the print

    Open Aging, tap Shoebox, Faded print or the 70s preset, then fine-tune Fade, Sunlight, Yellowing and Unevenness.

  3. 3

    Add wear

    Add film grain, dust, stains and creases until the print looks handled.

  4. 4

    Add a light leak

    Open Light Leak, choose Edge and Orange or Golden, keep Amount low.

  5. 5

    Save

    Save the photo, or save the edit as a recipe for a whole album.

Tips

  • Age the color before you add wear; damage on a modern-looking color gives it away.
  • Creases look real on portraits and family photos, less so on landscapes.
  • Add a date stamp from the right era to finish the story.
  • Compare with the before/after divider to avoid overdoing it.

Questions people also ask

How do I make a picture look 100 years old?

Use a black and white look like Mju 35, raise Fade and Yellowing for a sepia tone, add dust, stains and creases, and drop the date stamp.

Can I make a photo look old and scratched?

Halatto has no separate scratch tool. For a worn, handled print, use Aging with dust, creases and stains, raise Fade and Yellowing, and add a light leak. Together they give the marks and wear of an old print.

Can I undo the aging later?

Yes. Halatto keeps your edits, so you can reopen the photo and change or remove any effect.

Does this damage the original photo?

No. The edited version is saved; the original stays in your library.
